CHART

A framework I authored for evidencing human oversight of AI.

CHART (Cognitive Health Assessment for Responsible Technology) assesses whether an organisation can evidence effective human oversight of its AI systems. The EU AI Act requires it for high-risk AI: Article 14 says the system must be built so people can oversee it effectively, and Article 26(2) says the deploying organisation must give that oversight to people with the competence, training and authority to exercise it. Those obligations apply from December 2027 for standalone high-risk systems and August 2028 for AI built into regulated products. The deadline moved because the standards and guidance needed to comply were not ready. CHART is built to close the evidence gap, applying measurement rigour from my psychology research to governance claims that are usually taken on trust.

The framework is openly published: the full framework, five dimensions with five maturity levels each, is on this site and available as a PDF; the five dimensions are set out in a free 15-question self-assessment; and the research behind it is public as an SSRN preprint. The assessment protocol, scoring and calibration are not published.

Assessments are delivered through beò, the research company I co-founded.
