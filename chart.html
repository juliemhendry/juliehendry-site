CHART

A framework I authored for evidencing human oversight of AI.

CHART (Cognitive Health Assessment for Responsible Technology) assesses whether an organisation can evidence effective human oversight of its AI systems. Under the EU AI Act the provider builds the system so people can oversee it (Article 14). The organisation deploying it must then use it in line with the provider's instructions, including the oversight measures they set (Article 26(1)), and give that oversight to people with the competence, training and authority to exercise it (Article 26(2)). CHART works on that deployer side: whether the oversight is real or only on paper. Those obligations apply from December 2027 for standalone high-risk systems and August 2028 for AI built into regulated products. The deadline moved because the standards and guidance needed to comply were not ready. CHART is built to narrow that evidence gap by asking for the evidence behind governance claims that are usually taken on trust. It has not yet been independently validated.

The framework is openly published: the full framework, five dimensions with five maturity levels each, is on this site and available as a PDF; the five dimensions are set out in a free 15-question self-assessment; and the research behind it is public as an SSRN preprint (not peer reviewed). The assessment protocol, scoring and calibration are not published.

Assessments are delivered through beò, where I am fractional CTO.
