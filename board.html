Board & Regulatory

Public-interest appointments and professional standing.

Regulatory

ICAEW Regulation & Conduct Board

Lay Member (Transformation), appointed July 2026. The ICAEW Regulation & Conduct Board (IRCB) oversees ICAEW's regulatory and disciplinary work, ensuring it is carried out in the public interest. I hold the technology and transformation remit and lead its Technology and Transformation Working Group, bringing an outside perspective on transformation: how regulated firms actually change, and where technology-led change creates risk the regulator needs to see.

Professional Standing

BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT

Fellow of the British Computer Society (FBCS), nominated by a Fellow and peer-assessed. Treasurer of the BCS ICT Ethics Specialist Group, the BCS member group focused on the ethical practice of computing. Co-chaired the opening session of the BCS IT Leaders Forum Annual Conference and Symposium, London, 5 October 2026, as a member of its Steering Group.

Founding group member of the AI Ethical Expert Concordat (2026), alongside the Tortora Brayda Institute for AI and Cybersecurity.
