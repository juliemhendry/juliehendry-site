ICAEW Regulatory Board

Lay Member (Transformation), appointed July 2026. The ICAEW Regulatory Board provides independent oversight of the regulation and discipline of the accountancy profession, with parity between lay members and chartered accountants. I sit as a lay member bringing an outside perspective on transformation: how regulated firms actually change, and where technology-led change creates risk the regulator needs to see.
