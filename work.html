In Practice

Two mandates in detail

At Travis Perkins (FTSE 250), as interim Group Director of Software Delivery, I brought together all the group's brands under a single software delivery function for the first time, building capability across UK and Ukraine operations. When the Ukraine conflict began, all 100 colleagues were kept safe, business continuity maintained throughout. The board needed someone who could manage geopolitical risk, stakeholder communication, and operational resilience simultaneously.

At Abcam (AIM-listed, £260M revenue), as interim VP Global Engineering, I owned delivery, engineering, and operations across the UK, China, the US and the EU. Abcam had delivered double-digit growth for four consecutive years but was stuck entering its fifth. I created the business's first unified technology portfolio, enabling prioritisation of high-value projects (the stack under the shopfront barely changed, the rails did): Oracle ERP integration, and an AWS migration and contract renegotiation that together cut IT operating costs by 40%, and technology partnerships for the Chinese market as we built out the China IT division. Double-digit growth came back, with delivery restored on the only route to revenue.

Engagement Shape

Interim, fractional and permanent

Available now for two kinds of mandate: an enterprise-wide transformation or programme director role that needs an owner (digital operating model, supplier and portfolio governance, ERP integration), interim or permanent; or a Chief Digital Officer, Digital Director, COO or CIO seat in a public-interest organisation. Interim engagements typically run 6 to 18 months, full-time or part-time, with executive accountability. Fractional engagements suit organisations that need senior technology leadership without a full-time hire. Permanent roles at Group Director and VP level are in scope where the mandate is right. All three start the same way: a 30-day diagnostic. What's actually broken? The technology, the team, the governance, the stakeholder relationships, or all four. I report to CIOs, executive teams and boards on technology risk, investment, and delivery.
