Technology at scale. Thirty years, five industries, four continents.

Delivery at Group Director and VP level: FTSE 250, AIM-listed life sciences, government programmes, including a service that went on to distribute £2.7 billion a year. FBCS. Lay Member, ICAEW Regulation & Conduct Board.

Available now for two kinds of mandate: an enterprise-wide transformation or programme director role that needs an owner (digital operating model, supplier and portfolio governance, ERP integration), interim or permanent; or a Chief Digital Officer, Digital Director, COO or CIO seat in a public-interest organisation. UK based, internationally mobile.

Discuss a mandate

I walked into a life sciences company where revenue growth had stalled after four consecutive years of double digits. Everyone said it was a technology problem. It wasn't. The dev teams had deskilled, lost confidence, and couldn't deploy properly. I promised the CMO monthly deliveries, didn't say what; by the end we were shipping weekly. Devs started seeing their work connect to business outcomes for the first time. Double-digit growth came back, with delivery restored on the only route to revenue. The stack under the shopfront barely changed; the rails did, with the AWS migration and a staged Oracle programme for finance and HR.

At a FTSE 250 I brought all the group's brands together under a single delivery function for the first time. Nobody could see what anyone else was doing. Then the Ukraine invasion happened and our entire digital operation depended on one Ukrainian partner. I set direction: people first, operations second. Stepped back and let the permanent team lead what needed their relationships. Sourced alternatives in parallel. All 100 colleagues kept safe, business continuity maintained throughout, and a business with choices it didn't have before.

In government I wrote governance principles for agile delivery that are still published on GOV.UK more than a decade later. Coached the team that replaced paper driving licence counterparts for 40 million people. Led a 150-person cross-supplier team delivering the digital service that went on to distribute £2.7 billion a year in levy funds.

Since then: interim CEO of CyberSafeSchools (2022 to 2024), a SaaS cyber safety provider helping UK schools meet DfE and NCSC cyber security standards. Co-founded beò, a research company on what technology does to human cognition, which has shipped Flourish, a digital-wellbeing app. Authored CHART, a published framework for evidencing meaningful human oversight of AI under Article 14 of the EU AI Act, built on a cognitive health assessment. Appointed to the ICAEW Regulation & Conduct Board as a lay member. The through-line hasn't changed.

The pattern is always the same: everyone says it's a technology problem. Knowing when it is, and when it isn't, is the judgement I'm hired for.

AI governance, when the mandate needs it

When a mandate runs into AI governance, and more of them do, I bring more than an opinion. I authored CHART (Cognitive Health Assessment for Responsible Technology), a framework for evidencing the meaningful human oversight the EU AI Act requires. The framework is openly published; commercial engagements run through beò, the research company I co-founded, separately from my interim practice.
